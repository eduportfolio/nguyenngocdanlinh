Undergraduate Research
CONSUMER BEHAVIOR · 2026

Factors Influencing Young Consumers' Intention to Choose Green Products: Evidence from Hanoi

This study investigates the determinants of green product purchase intentions among young urban consumers in Hanoi, Vietnam — one of Southeast Asia's fastest-growing consumer markets. Using a quantitative survey methodology and structural equation modeling (SEM), the research identifies and ranks the relative influence of environmental attitudes, social norms, perceived quality, price sensitivity, and eco-label awareness.

Methodology Survey + SEM / Quantitative
Sample Young consumers, Hanoi
Field Consumer Behavior · Sustainability
Key Findings
  • Environmental attitude and subjective norms are the strongest predictors of green purchase intent
  • Price remains a significant barrier even among eco-conscious respondents
  • Eco-label literacy is lower than expected, suggesting a marketing gap
  • Peer influence amplifies green purchasing intent, especially through social media
